## Blocker: the entry DE is empty after two v9 runs (17:15 and 17:31). Two causes, measured with a one-off count query on
`ent.Lead_Salesforce` (leads created in the last 3 days at quote step 5.0/5.5, record type 0123h000000VF9DAAW):

| all v8 filters without the suppression | 51 |
| all v8 filters with the suppression | 0 |

1. **`MobilePhone` is never populated on Lead** (0 of 3,501 leads at step 5/5.5 in the last 90 days; `Phone` is set on 83%,
   `Mobile_Country_Code__c` on 100%). The journey texts `Mobile_Country_Code__c` (its mobile default). So the eligibility outcome
   `MobilePhone is not null` qualifies nobody. Fix: replace it with `Mobile_Country_Code__c is not equal to 1`. That field is a formula ("1" + Phone), so a blank
   Phone yields exactly "1" (605 of 605 blank-Phone leads in 90 days); "is not null" would pass them. With the corrected rule
   112 of the 130 step-5 leads of the last 3 days are eligible in Salesforce. The MobilePhone column in the DE and v9 is harmless but useless.
2. **The 14-day email suppression is a flow limiter, not a bug.** `Emails_Sent_Last_14_Days` (query `emails_sent_last_14_days`,
   step 1 of automation AC_QuoteSteps, every 15 min) holds every email address that received any email except PreQuote in the last
   14 days. It exists so a person who re-quotes under a new Lead Id (new Id each requote, sometimes within the hour) cannot re-enter,
   and it also keeps one quote-step journey per person. The 1-of-115 snapshot is misleading: it counts leads still pending in the
   3-day window, and most of those were already emailed by this journey. The real flow is the first-touch send count: v16 sent ES1/E1
   to **443 distinct subscribers in the 29 days to 2026-11-03, about 15 a day** (counted from `_Sent`). Keep the rule; size the test
   on 15 entrants a day (see pass 2 note below).

The scoring-date gate itself is fine: 107 of 115 recent leads carry a scoring date in the synced Lead table.

## Volume note for the read (pass 2)
At 15 entrants a day and a 50 / 50 split: ~7 learning leads a day, ~2.5 per arm, ~75 per arm a month. At a 10% purchase rate the
"150 purchases per arm" trigger for v18 is ~1,500 leads per arm, i.e. well over a year. Six months gives ~450 learning leads per arm
and ~45 purchases per arm: enough to see a 5-point lift between arms only if it is large, and enough to audit the routing. Options,
in order of preference: (a) keep the suppression and read at six months as directional, retrain on what is there; (b) scope the
suppression to this journey's own first-touch emails (ES1/E1) so leads that the earlier quote-step journeys emailed can still enter,
which can raise the flow only to the eligible creation rate, **548 leads in 28 days, 19.6 a day** (Salesforce, step 5/5.5,
   country code <> 1, no opt-outs, not purchased/declined/saved), so at most +5 a day, and it stacks journeys on the same person;
   re-quotes are 8% of step-5 leads (1,071 leads, 983 distinct emails in 28 days), which the suppression correctly removes; (c) leave the learning slice at
50 / 50 longer than six months.
