## Gaps found, in priority order

1. **Scoring runs daily; leads enter the journey within minutes of creation.** Entrants' median age at entry is
   under an hour (p95 2.4 hours, max 3 days). The legacy pipeline scored hourly and still only 65% of entrants
   carried a score at entry (median 1.4 h from creation to first score). With the new chain on a daily cadence,
   almost no lead would be scored when the journey decides, the formula would read blank scores as zero, and every
   lead with a phone would route to `SMS + Email`. Fix, in order of preference:
   - Schedule `ChannelScoringSet`, the three prediction jobs and `ChannelScores` **hourly**, staggered 15 minutes
     apart, with the enrichment hourly after them (the legacy chain ran this way: its feature transform held only
     the 18 leads of the last hour).
   - In journey v17, lengthen the entry wait before the channel decision from 5 minutes to **3 hours**, so the
     hourly chain has landed for nearly every entrant. The first email already waits on send-time optimisation,
     so the customer-facing delay is small.
   - Longer term: score at creation with the models' Flow action (Einstein Studio exposes custom models to Flow),
     which removes the race entirely. Not needed for v17.
2. **Journey `SMS Creation` is still publishing** (Once and done) and the automation still has it as step 2. Since
   the Apex sync went live last night, every first-text lead gets two `SMS__c` rows: one from the journey (no key)
   and one from Apex (keyed). Remove step 2 from the automation and stop the journey now rather than later.
3. **Legacy scoring still runs** (`LeadMultiChannelScoring` transform ran 20:06 UTC and the three legacy prediction
   jobs still fill `Lead_Multi_Channel_Scoring__dlm`, 18 rows). Harmless with the enrichment off, but it burns
   credits and invites confusion. Deactivate the three legacy models' jobs and the `LeadMultiChannelScoring` and
   `ChannelPredictionTrainingData` transforms.
4. **`Email_Response_Scoring_Set__cio` is now redundant** (the transform replaced it as prediction input).
   Deactivate it. The five training insights run daily but are read monthly; set them to weekly to save compute,
   keeping `Channel_Uplift_Training_Set` daily once the journey writes arms.
5. **Scored At -> `Last_Channel_Scoring_Date__c`** was missing from the first enrichment sync and was added at
   20:38; confirm on the next hourly sync that the date lands (a lead written today should show today's timestamp).
6. **`Predicted_Channel__c` is not in the Lead DMO** (formula fields do not come through the CRM connector). Nothing
   reads it from Data Cloud today; the journey gets it through Marketing Cloud Connect. If the readout ever needs
   it, recompute from the three scores in SQL.
7. **Interim behaviour in v16**: the new value `Call + SMS + Email` has no outcome in the v16 decision; it falls
   into the `SMS + Email` outcome because that decision uses "contains", so those leads get texts but no call until
   v17 is published. No lead is lost.
