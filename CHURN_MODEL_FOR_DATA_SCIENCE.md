# Voluntary churn model (v4): what we built, on what data, and why

For the Data Science team. Written 2026-11-02 by HiRoad lifecycle marketing. This is the technical
companion to `CHURN_PROGRAM_SUMMARY.md`; the dated validation reports, the transform definitions and the
validation script are under `journeys/ChurnRiskActivation/`. Everything here lives in the Salesforce org
(blueowl-prod) and its Data Cloud instance; the model is a Model Builder (Einstein Studio) gradient-boosted
classifier. We would like your review of the design, the evaluation, and the open issues at the end.

## 1. Why a new model

Two churn scores already existed on Contact (`Churn_Risk_Score__c`, `Normalized_Churn_Risk_Score__c`,
Data Cloud models "Predicted Did Cancel"). Both were trained on all cancellations. On 2026-10-16 Insurance
(Jennifer Kellett, Sandy Betz) ruled that retention effort must not target non-payment churn and that
credit-based signals stay out of retention targeting. Non-payment is 43% of cancellations, so the existing
scores ranked highest exactly the customers Insurance does not want pursued. We also found the existing
training set was not point-in-time (61,215 rows, 90% positive because positives were all-time cancels;
features for leavers measured up to the cancel date, for stayers up to today). Out of time, the normalized
score had AUC 0.59 on all cancels and 0.51 on voluntary churn; tenure alone had 0.68.

The program was rebuilt from the label up. Target: preventable voluntary cancellation within 60 days.

## 2. Label

From `Contact.Cancellation_Reason__c`, 2026 cancellations to mid-October (n = 7,765):

| Group | Reasons | Share | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preventable voluntary | Due to high premium, Replacement of insurance carrier, Insured request - other, App issue / UX, poor customer service, poor claims service | 28% | **positive** (`Label_60__c`) |
| Non-payment | Non-payment of premium | 43% | censored |
| Company cancel | underwriting / company-initiated | 20% | censored |
| Unpreventable | sold vehicle, no longer driving, moved out of state, total loss | 9% | censored |

Censored means the row is removed from training and holdout when the cancellation falls inside the 60-day
window (`Censored__c = 1`), so those customers are neither negatives nor positives. A 90-day label is
carried but unused. Base rate on uncensored rows is about 5% (4.9% on the current holdout); roughly 230
preventable voluntary cancels a month.

## 3. Training set construction (point-in-time)

Built as a Data Cloud batch transform (`ChurnPITTrainingSet`, generated by
`transforms/build_transform_json.py`, 181 nodes). Design:

- **Snapshots**: the first of every month, July 2024 to the latest month whose 60-day window has closed
  (26 to 27 snapshots). A calendar table drives it; snapshots activate automatically once closed.
- **Unit**: Contact x snapshot. A contact is in the book at a snapshot if policy effective date and
  CreatedDate are on or before it and cancel date is null or after it. 237,547 rows in the latest run
  (about 7,100 to 9,500 per snapshot). Row key = contact id + snapshot key.
- **Features are windowed to dates strictly on or before the snapshot.** An audit column
  `Max_Feature_Date__c` holds the latest event date any feature used; it never exceeds the snapshot date
  (zero violations on every run).
- **Train / holdout split by time, not at random.** Train = snapshots through 2026-04 (v4: 166,781 rows,
  uncensored, driving-decline rows removed); holdout = 2026-05 through 2026-08 (v4: 24,450 rows, 1,209
  positives). Model Builder's own random-split CV is not quoted because the same contact appears in many
  snapshots on both sides; it reports 0.67 where the time-split holdout gives 0.64.
- Views for each version (`ChurnPITTrainV4`, `ChurnPITHoldoutV4`) drop the columns that version must not
  see and apply row filters; the training transform itself is shared.

## 4. Features, by source

All joins are to the snapshot, point-in-time. Coverage figures are the share of training rows with data.

| Family | Source object (Salesforce) | Columns (v4) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tenure and term | Contact | `Tenure_Days`, `Tenure_Bucket`, `Policy_Days`, `Days_Into_Term_Est`, `Days_To_Term_End_Est`, `Mailing_State` | Term position is estimated from the effective date and a 6-month term, because real term dates exist only as current values |
| Renewal offers | `Renewal_Offer__c` (new object; Account flow captures each offer since 2026-10-20, backfilled from field history to 2025-10) | `Renewal_Offer_Rate`, `Renewal_Offer_Change_Dollars`, `Renewal_Offer_Change_Pct`, `Renewal_Rating_Version`, `Days_Since_Renewal_Offer_PIT` | Latest offer issued in the 60 days before the snapshot. Covers about 22% of rows from 2026-02. The strongest single driver: offered increase over $20/month cancels at 11 to 21% against 4% |
| Billing (price history) | `Bill__c` | `Bill_Net_Latest`, `Bill_Gross_Latest`, `Bill_Net_Change_3m`, `Bill_Net_Change_3m_Pct`, `Bill_Count` | Net premium per invoice; 70% coverage. **No payment-behaviour fields** (notices, late, past due) are used. `Bill_Count` (re-issued invoices) correlates with non-pay; kept by decision, see section 7 |
| Driving telemetry | `Monthly_Driving_Stat__c` (219,710 monthly cycles since 2023-05) | `Drv_Score_Latest`, `Drv_Score_Trend`, `Drv_Trips_Latest`, `Drv_Miles_Latest`, `Drv_Discount_Latest`, `Drv_Discount_Change`, `Drv_Cycles`, `Days_Since_Last_Cycle`, `Drv_Trips_Change_Pct` | Joined on customer number; about 45% coverage. Discount columns are dollars of monthly earned discount, not points. The feed carries primary named insureds only since 2026-09 |
| Savings history (new in v4) | `Savings_History__c` (new object; ANA-6320 warehouse backfill 2023-05 to 2026-09, 363,264 customer-month rows; `Savings_History_Capture` Account flow writes each cycle as it closes) | `Sav_Pct_Latest`, `Sav_Pct_Change_3m`, `Sav_Discount_Latest`, `Sav_Discount_Change_3m`, `Sav_Lifetime_At_Snap`, `Sav_Per_Tenure_Month`, `Sav_Standard_Latest`, `Sav_Net_Latest`, `Sav_Effective_Rate_Latest`, `Sav_Earned_Latest`, `Sav_Disc_Months6`, `Sav_Cycles`, `Days_Since_Last_Savings_Cycle` | Point-in-time on cycle close; 52 to 58% coverage by year. `Sav_Earned_Latest` is the driving-earned discount fraction (0 to 0.5), a warehouse figure with no Salesforce source; see section 8 |
| App | `MobileApp__c` events | `App_Events_30d`, `App_Events_90d`, `Days_Since_Last_App_Event` | 62% of rows have app history |
| Email | Individual Email Results (Marketing Cloud sync) | `Email_Open_Rate_90d`, `Days_Since_Last_Email_Event` | Joined on lower-cased email address, not the contact lookup, because most customer-period sends sit on the converted Lead record (87k vs 5k recoverable sends in 2024). Send, open and click **counts** were removed after v1: the count of sends was the non-pay reminder journey's volume, a payment proxy |
| Support | `Zen_Desk_Ticket__c` | `Tickets_90d`, `Days_Since_Last_Ticket` | From 2025 |

v4 uses 45 predictors: the 31 above from v3 plus the 13 savings columns and `Drv_Trips_Change_Pct`.
Exact select and deselect lists: `BUILD_GUIDE.md`, "Version 4".

**Deliberately excluded.** Payment Stability Score, credit score, notice counts, late and past-due rates
(Insurance decision). `Driver_Role__c`: populated only on contacts still active, so it leaks the label
(every populated row is a negative). Current-value Contact fields (`Lifetime_Savings__c`,
`Telematics_Discount__c`, real term dates, `Days_To_Renewal__c`) because they describe today, not the
snapshot. Lead attributes and post-purchase re-quotes: tested, no voluntary signal (completed re-quotes
mark lapse-and-rebuy, mostly non-pay).

**Row exclusion (v4).** Insurance ruled on 2026-10-29 that customers whose driving score dropped and whose
discount fell as a result must not receive retention outreach. `Drv_Decline_Flag` (display score fell 5 or
more points against about three cycles earlier while trips did not fall by half) marks 4 to 9% of rows by
year; those rows are removed from train and holdout so the model is fit on the population the journey can
act on. The removed slice cancels at 7.2% against 6.7%.

## 5. Evaluation method

`scripts/validate_holdout.py` pulls the prediction job's output and the holdout through Data Cloud SQL,
joins on row key, and reports: AUC with a bootstrap standard error; AUC of tenure alone on the same rows as
the floor; AUC within tenure buckets and by cancellation reason; deciles and lift; calibration; top
contributor mix; and a compliance proxy (share of the top decile with a non-pay notice in the prior 90 days
against the book). Pass lines set before training: AUC at least 0.68 and at least tenure + 0.05; at least
0.60 inside each tenure bucket; high premium 0.70, carrier switch 0.65, insured request 0.60; monotonic
deciles with top decile at least 2.5x base.

Versions are compared on identical rows by joining their score objects on row key. A model must not be
scored on an older holdout object that lacks some of its inputs; that handicaps it.

## 6. Results

| Version | Change | Holdout AUC | Tenure floor | Top decile | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| existing normalized score | all-cancel label | 0.51 on the preventable label | 0.56 | | unusable for this target |
| v1 | 21 predictors: tenure, term, app, email counts, tickets, offers | 0.579 | 0.560 | 1.8x, not monotonic | top contributor = email sends = non-pay reminder volume |
| v2 | + driving, email by address, state; send counts out | 0.599 | 0.560 | 1.9x, monotonic | only the under-6-month bucket passes 0.60 |
| v3 | + bill price history; email counts out | 0.627 (25,419 rows) | 0.560 | 2.0x | first lift outside the renewal window; in production since 2026-10-20 |
| **v4** | + savings history, trips change; current-value savings out; decline rows out | **0.639 (24,450 rows); v3 on the same rows 0.628** | 0.564 | **2.28x (11.3% vs 4.9%)** | paired bootstrap gain +0.003 to +0.020, v4 ahead in 200 of 200 resamples |

v4 within tenure: under 6 months 0.662, 6 to 12 months 0.618, 1 to 2 years 0.630, 2 years and over
0.601 (v3: 0.652 / 0.601 / 0.606 / 0.598). By reason: high premium 0.665, carrier switch 0.611, insured
request 0.612. Deciles, top first: 11.29, 6.54, 5.19, 5.56, 4.79, 4.62, 3.64, 3.15, 2.70, 1.96. Top
importance: `Sav_Earned_Latest` 9.0%, `Bill_Net_Latest` 6.4%, `Bill_Gross_Latest` 5.1%,
`Bill_Net_Change_3m_Pct` 3.9%, `Bill_Count` 3.6%, then term position and app recency.

Neither v3 nor v4 reaches the 0.68 or 2.5x lines. On the renewal window (customers with an offer in the
prior 60 days) every version lands at 0.61 to 0.62, and the one-line rule "offered increase over $20"
lands at 0.614, so there the model mostly rediscovers the offer. The step function in the offer itself
(uncensored, 11,725 offers since 2025-12): decrease or up to +$20 cancels at 4.1 to 4.3% in 60 days,
+$20 to $30 at 11.0%, +$30 to $50 at 11.6%, +$50 and up at 20.9%.

## 7. Known weaknesses and decisions already taken

- **Compliance proxy.** v3's top decile carries more non-pay history than the book (35% vs 23%). Causes:
  `Bill_Count` (re-issued invoices) and premium level/increase, which correlate with non-pay but are price,
  not payment. The program owner decided to ship v3 as is; the journey excludes anyone with a non-pay notice
  in 90 days at entry, so targeting is governed there. Not re-run for v4.
- **Term position is estimated.** Real term dates are current-value only. A historical term table from the
  policy system would replace `Days_*_Est`.
- **Offers cover 22% of rows** and only from 2025-11; 12 more months of captured offers will help most.
- **Household members without an invoice** (about 30% of rows have no recent bill) score high and do cancel
  more. Legitimate but worth knowing when reading contributor output.
- **Repeated contacts across snapshots** inflate any random-split metric; only time-split figures are quoted.
- **Savings and driving coverage is about half of rows**; missingness is informative and the model uses it.

## 8. The production dependency we want your eyes on

`Sav_Earned_Latest`, v4's top feature, is the warehouse's earned discount fraction. No Salesforce field
holds it, so the capture flow reconstructs it: Monthly_Driving_Stat earned dollars divided by the
discountable base (discount dollars over rated percent). On backfill cycles since mid-2025 this matches
the extract within 0.01 on 83% and within 0.02 on 88% (median error 0; misses are whole-dollar rounding on
small premiums). Since the 2026-11-01 batch the flow has written 237 cycle rows; 175 carry a discount and
115 of those (66%) carry the reconstructed fraction. The gap is cycles with no matching driving record,
and the driving feed carries primary named insureds only since 2026-09, so production coverage will sit
below the backfill's roughly 90%. The model saw missing values in training ("no driving data"), so this is
a degradation, not a break, but it is a distribution shift between training and scoring. The clean fix is a
recurring feed of the engine's own value from Data Analytics (ANA-6320 owner: Liz Pund).

## 9. Production pipeline (v3 live, v4 ready)

1. Nightly: Account flows capture renewal offers and savings cycles; CRM streams land them in Data Cloud.
2. Daily: `ChurnPITScoringSet` rebuilds the active book as a single current snapshot with the same
   features (no labels).
3. Predict job `Voluntary Churn 60d v3 - Production Scoring` writes score and top three contributors to
   `Voluntary_Churn_Score__dlm` (7,878 contacts on the first run).
4. Monthly on the 2nd: `scripts/write_back_scores.py` writes score, band (High = top decile, Moderate =
   next two), scored date, top factors, a rule-based primary reason (PRICE_SHOCK, DISCOUNT_EROSION,
   NEW_CUSTOMER, DISENGAGED, LOW_SAVINGS, GENERAL), offer change, discount change and driving score to
   eight Contact fields; the band cut points go to the retention journey's config table in Marketing Cloud.
5. Monthly on the 2nd: the training transform re-runs; a newly closed snapshot joins the holdout.
6. Promotion of v4: re-post the scoring transform with the savings columns, new predict job on v4, repoint
   the write-back, re-derive cut points. Pending the decision in section 8.

The score feeds a retention journey that enters customers about 27 days before term end when the new-term
offer exceeds $20 or 10% (tier 1) or the model places them in its top decile (tier 2), excludes recent
non-pay, and sends an email and an app push. Insurance has ruled out a long randomized holdout, so the read
will be a year-over-year term-end cohort comparison plus a cut-line comparison (`journeys/RE_ChurnRetention/TEST_PLAN.md`).

## 10. What we are asking

1. Review the label and censoring choices, the point-in-time construction and the time-split evaluation.
   Anything you would do differently before we rely on it for a cohort read?
2. A view on the compliance proxy question: is "price correlates with non-pay" an acceptable reason to keep
   premium-level features, given the journey-level exclusion?
3. Whether Data Science could own a recurring earned-discount feed, or the model altogether, on your own
   stack. The training set is a plain table (`Churn_PIT_Training_Set__dll`, 78 columns, row key, labels,
   features, audit date) that exports cleanly.
4. Ideas for the remaining gap to 0.68: historical term dates, the policy system's renewal pricing
   inputs, claims, and anything you already hold on the same customer number.
